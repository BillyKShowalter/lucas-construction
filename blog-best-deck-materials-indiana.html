Best Deck Materials for Indiana Weather

Published April 2026 · Outdoor Living · Lucas Construction

Pressure-treated lumber

Affordable and widely available. Requires regular sealing or staining to last. Good choice if budget is a priority and you're comfortable with periodic maintenance.

Composite decking

Low maintenance and durable, composite boards resist rot and are often warranty-backed. They cost more up front but reduce lifetime maintenance costs.

Hardwood and specialty options

Exotic hardwoods or capped composites offer premium looks and longevity but come at a price. Choose materials that match the expected use and local climate.
